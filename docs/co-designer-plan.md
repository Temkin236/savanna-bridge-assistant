## Session rules

1. Confirm the device, browser, language, access preferences, date, and time.
2. Ask for access preferences, not diagnosis or medical history.
3. Give the goal without step-by-step instructions.
4. Record whether the task was completed unaided, where the participant paused, and what they expected to happen.
5. Do not store the participant's name, phone number, diagnosis, account data, recording, or message history in Git.
6. Obtain explicit consent before recording anything. No recording is required for milestone evidence.
